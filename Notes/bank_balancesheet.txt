So my simple bank works more or less like this:

- People save money there (this side will not be visible by the player, but the amount can be altered by macroeconomic factors like economic shocks 
    or the raise of the reference interest rate by the national central bank). So in the beginning we have a certain amount of money to lend as a bank. 
    This money, let's say, maybe having 1,000,000 euro in the bank, is both an asset (potentially) and a liability, and asset because it can technically 
    be used to be lend to people and a liability because it is the money of others so the bank owes money to these people, it is debt of the bank. 
- We have two interest rates, savings rate and lending rates. In my game we will assume a standard saving rate, and the lending rate, will be 
    determined given the score cards (and maybe other factors), but the lowest lending rate will be higher that the saving rate (because that is how 
    banks fundamentally make money, from interest). 
- Economic factors are present in the game, like the reference interest rate, if it moves, then I also move my saving interest rate 
    (should be done automatically) and the lending rates should be also readjusted (we can leave to the player this readjustment). 
    Also another thing is the minimum legal requirement called in Spanish "encaje legal", which is the minimum level of capital that the bank 
    should keep, that is if it is 10%, 10% of 1,000,000 is 100,000, that cannot be lend and should be kept saved.
- Economic shocks can happen in the game, not very elaborated, but maybe like a shock in which a lot of people starting taking their savings out 
    of the bank, thus reducing the capital available of the bank, or vice versa. This should not be able for the player to interact, the shocks 
    will just be displayed on the screen, but they will have an impact in the Balance sheet of the bank.
